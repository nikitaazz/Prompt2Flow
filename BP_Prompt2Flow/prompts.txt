Business Processes in Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) Production
Stage 1 — Raw Material Reception and Incoming Quality Control.
The raw materials received include sand, gypsum, cement, quicklime, and aluminium paste.
Stage 2 — Preparation of the Semi-Finished Product (Sand Slurry).
For this purpose, river sand and gypsum are fed into a wet grinding mill, where they are ground, mixed with water, and transferred to storage tanks equipped with continuous mixing systems.
Stage 3 — Raw Material Dosing and Mixing.
The components to be dosed include sand slurry, recycled slurry, water, cement, lime, and aluminium paste. After dosing, all components are successively fed into a mixer, where they are thoroughly mixed. The resulting mixture is then poured into a casting mould.
Stage 4 — Rising and Pre-Curing of the AAC Cake.
During this stage, hydrogen is released as a result of a chemical reaction, causing the mixture to expand to approximately twice its original volume. During pre-curing, the cake gains sufficient strength to allow the mould to be dismantled and the cake to proceed to the cutting stage.
Stage 5 — Cutting.
The cake is cut horizontally and vertically to achieve the specified geometrical dimensions.
Stage 6 — Thermal Treatment (Autoclaving).
This stage takes place in autoclaves, where the cut cakes are loaded. The process begins with vacuuming, followed by the introduction of steam for thermal treatment. The pressure is then increased to 12 bar and the temperature rises to 200 °C. After the holding period, the pressure is gradually released. During this stage, the chemical reaction is completed, and the AAC products achieve their final strength.
Stage 7 — Separation and Packaging.
If necessary, the cake is separated into individual products. The finished products are then sorted and packaged on wooden pallets.

Business Process: Frying an Egg in a Pan
First, take a frying pan, either non-stick or regular. If it is made of cast iron, add a drop of oil. If it does not have a non-stick coating, use plenty of oil. Put the pan on the stove and wait until it heats up. Crack an egg into the pan without dropping the shell in. Add salt and pepper to taste, then cover the pan with a lid. Wait a couple of minutes, turn off the heat, and scrape the egg off the pan.
